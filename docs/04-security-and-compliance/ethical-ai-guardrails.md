# KAIRO: Anti-Surveillance Policy & Ethical AI Guardrails

> **Domain:** Security, Privacy & Compliance  
> **Document ID:** KAIRO-SEC-ETHICS  
> **Directive:** Workplace Trust & Knowledge Continuity Without Surveillance

---

## 1. Anti-Surveillance Guardrails

> **Strict Product Directives:**
> 1. **No Developer Ranking:** Kairo never compares engineers or suggests "who is the best developer".
> 2. **No Velocity / Output Metrics:** The data model strictly excludes lines of code written, commits per day, or active working hours.
> 3. **Context Continuity Only:** System outputs are strictly designed to reconstruct work state for seamless project transitions.

---

## 2. Citation Grounding & Transparency

* Every synthesized claim must include an inline verifiable citation (`[Source: Jira-421]`, `[Source: PR #834]`).
* Unverifiable statements are pruned by output verification filters before rendering to users.
